MFOOTRECAP WEBSITE — STARTER PACK

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html: website content
- styles.css: design, colours and responsive layout
- script.js: mobile menu and current year
- assets/: put your Canva logo here

PREVIEW ON YOUR ACER LAPTOP
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open the MFootRecap-Website folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. Keep the internet on for the optional Google Fonts.

ADD YOUR CANVA LOGO
1. In Canva, download your logo as a PNG (transparent background is ideal).
2. Copy the downloaded image into the assets folder.
3. Rename it exactly: logo.png
4. Refresh the website. The logo will appear in the header and as the browser tab icon.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- The three story cards are placeholders; replace them with real articles.
- The site links to Instagram @mfootrecap.ig and mfootbusiness@gmail.com.
- This is a static starter website. It can be hosted online, but the current version does not have a publishing dashboard or database yet.
- Do not publish private details. Only use contact details you intend to make public.
